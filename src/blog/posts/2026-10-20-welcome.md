Factories around the world still run on Japanese automation: Yaskawa servos, Mitsubishi PLCs, Keyence sensors. When one of those parts fails and the model is no longer made, finding a genuine replacement outside Japan can take weeks.

HUMI exists to make that easier. From Fukuoka, we source industrial parts across Japan and ship them to maintenance teams abroad.

## What this blog will cover

- News from the Japanese manufacturing and automation industry
- Robotics, including humanoids, and the components behind them
- Practical notes on keeping older equipment running

Posts are written from the shop floor, not the press release. If there's a topic you'd like us to look into, [send us a note](/#contact).
